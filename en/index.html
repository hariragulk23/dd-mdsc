  1. 01 · Score

    80 points or more in cupping

    Trained tasters score each coffee out of 100 with a common protocol. From 80, it is specialty.

    You know where it comes from

    Country, region, farm, altitude, variety and process. The more specific, the better the traceability.

    It is roasted to show its own flavour, not to hide it. Nothing to do with torrefacto, which has sugar.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most about specialty coffee.

Another question? Write to us →
01What is the difference between specialty coffee and ordinary coffee?

The cupping score (80 points or more, according to the SCA), traceable origin, and a roast designed to show the flavour of the bean, not to hide it.

02Why does it cost more?

There is more work at every step: selective harvesting, careful processing, small batches and a recent roast. And often a fairer price for the people who grow it.

03Do I need an expensive coffee machine to brew it at home?

No. A V60-style dripper, a scale and freshly ground coffee are enough to start. Try the calculator in our guides.

04Do you sell coffee online?

Our online shop is coming soon, with coffee whole bean or ground when you order. For businesses and larger volumes we also work with roasted coffee tailored to your needs.

It depends on the project. Tell us what you need and we will send you a no-obligation quote.
