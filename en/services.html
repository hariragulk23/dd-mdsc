Questions about the services

What people ask us before starting a project.

Another question? Write to us →
01Do you only work in Madrid?

We serve clients in Madrid and all over Spain. Our registered office is in Torrevieja (Alicante). Advice, supply and some courses can also be done in other cities. Ask us.

02Is there a minimum for green or roasted coffee?

It depends on the lot and the format. We tell you in the quote.

03Do the courses give a certificate?

Once the courses start, you will receive a certificate of attendance. For official SCA certifications we advise you on where to get them.

Do you have a coffee project in Madrid?

Tell us your idea and we will reply with the next steps.
