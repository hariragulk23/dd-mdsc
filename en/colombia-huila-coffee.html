Your recipe for this coffee

Choose your method and the number of cups. We give you the amounts.

Espresso

Recommended for this coffee.

  1. Grind fine just before brewing.
  2. Put 18 g in the portafilter and tamp it level.
  3. Extract about 36 g of espresso in 25 to 30 seconds.
  4. If it tastes sour, grind finer. If it is bitter, grind coarser.

V60 and filter

Recommended for this coffee.

  1. Rinse the filter with hot water.
  2. Add the medium-fine ground coffee.
  3. Pour twice as much water as coffee and wait 30 seconds.
  4. Pour the rest in slow circles.
  5. It should finish at around 3 minutes.

French press

Recommended for this coffee.

  1. Grind coarse.
  2. Pour in the water and stir.
  3. Put the lid on without pressing the plunger down and wait 4 minutes.
  4. Skim the foam off the top and press the plunger down slowly.

Moka pot

Recommended for this coffee.

  1. Fill the base with hot water up to the valve.
  2. Fill the basket with medium-fine ground coffee, level and without pressing.
  3. Medium heat, with the lid open.
  4. Take it off the heat when it starts to bubble and cool the base.

It depends on your moka pot

The size of the basket sets the amount: fill it level to the top.

AeroPress

It works too.

  1. Medium-fine ground coffee in the AeroPress.
  2. Pour in the water and stir gently.
  3. Wait a minute and a half.
  4. Press down slowly for about 30 seconds.

Questions about this coffee

01Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean stays fresh for longer. If you choose ground, we grind it when you order, with the grind for your method.

02How do I store it?

Close the bag tightly with its zip and keep it away from light and heat. More tips in our guides.

03When was it roasted?

We roast every week, in small batches. The lot and the best-before date are on the pack. If you would like to know the roast date, write to us.

04How do I order?

Add it to the cart, enter your postcode and pay securely by card with Stripe. You can also order on WhatsApp.

05How long does it take to arrive?

We prepare your order within 24 to 48 working hours and ship it to mainland Spain. Free shipping from €35; below that, €4.95. More details on shipping.
