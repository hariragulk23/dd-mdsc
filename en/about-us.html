About Madrid Specialty Coffee

Madrid Specialty Coffee is a specialty coffee brand. We bring together guides, services for coffee businesses and an online shop, coming soon.

What we do

  • Coffee guides and culture: we explain what specialty coffee is, how it is scored and how to brew it at home. See the guides and the journal.
  • Services for businesses: opening a café, creating a coffee brand, green coffee, roasted coffee for businesses, training in brewing methods, origin advice and a coffee bar for events. See the services. The roasting courses and tasting workshops are coming soon.
  • Online shop: coming soon.

Where we are

Our registered office is in Torrevieja (Alicante). The brand is aimed at Madrid and the whole of Spain. We do not have a physical shop open to the public.

Who is behind it

Madrid Specialty Coffee is a brand of THE COFFEE PROJECT OF 69 SL, a single-member limited company, tax ID (NIF) B19853563, with its registered address at Avenida Virgen del Carmen, 2, 3.º A, 03188 Torrevieja (La Mata), Alicante, Spain. It is registered in the Alicante Commercial Registry, sheet A-192887.

How we describe each coffee

On each coffee's page we state its origin, altitude, variety, process and SCA score, together with the food information. If you need any more details, ask us.
