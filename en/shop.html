Questions about the shop

Orders, shipping and grinds.

Another question? Write to us →
01How do I place an order?

Add your coffees to the cart, enter your postcode and pay securely by card with Stripe. You can also order on WhatsApp and we will confirm availability, shipping and payment.

02Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean stays fresh for longer. If you choose ground, we grind it when you order, with the grind for your method.

03How much does shipping cost?

Free shipping from €35. Below €35, shipping costs €4.95. For now we ship to mainland Spain.

04Do you sell to cafés and offices?

Yes. For larger volumes we offer roasted coffee for businesses.
