Cookie policy

This English text is a courtesy translation. The Spanish version is the legal text and prevails. Spanish version

Last updated: October 2026.

Summary

  • This website does not use analytics, advertising or social media cookies.
  • We do not have tools to track visits.
  • That is why we do not show a cookie notice. We only use technical storage that is necessary for the website to work.

What is stored in your browser

  • Shop cart: the products you add are stored in your browser's local storage. This means they are not lost when you change page. It is not sent to our servers until you pay. It is deleted when you empty the cart or clear your browser data.
  • Page transitions: a temporary piece of data that is deleted when you close the tab.

These data are strictly necessary for the service you request, so they do not require your consent.

External resources

The fonts and the animation libraries are loaded from our own server. They are not requested from other companies.

When you pay

When you pay, you go to Stripe's page, which uses its own technical and anti-fraud cookies to process the payment securely. See Stripe's cookie policy on its website.

Links to WhatsApp

The WhatsApp buttons take you to that app or to its website, which have their own policies.

How to delete stored data

You can delete it from your browser settings. If you do, the cart will be emptied.
