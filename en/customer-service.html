If you have a problem with your order

  1. Tell us what happened and what your order number is.
  2. If the product arrives damaged or wrong, attach photos of the product and the packaging.
  3. We reply as soon as possible and propose a solution: replacement, refund or another, as required by law.

Complaints and claims

  • You can submit your complaint (queja o reclamación) through any of the channels above. We deal with it and reply in writing if you ask us to.
  • If you do not agree with our reply, you can go to the Municipal Consumer Information Office (Oficina Municipal de Información al Consumidor, OMIC) in your town or to the consumer services of your autonomous community (comunidad autónoma).
  • You can also turn to the alternative consumer dispute resolution bodies (entidades de resolución alternativa de litigios en materia de consumo) on the list of the consumer authority, or go to the courts of your place of domicile.
